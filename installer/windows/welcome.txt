Converter turns images, audio, video, data, documents and 3D models into other
formats - entirely on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed,
and the app works offline.

Before it converts anything, it tells you whether the result will be lossless or
lossy, and why. Quality is never promised silently.

The app is installed for your user only (no administrator rights needed). It
appears in the Start menu, and you can also put a shortcut on the desktop on the
next page.
