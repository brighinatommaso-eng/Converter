Converter is installed.

Start it from the Start menu ("Converter"), or from the desktop shortcut if you
created one. You can also pin it to the taskbar from there.

Settings, presets and conversion history are stored in %APPDATA%\Converter. They
belong to you: uninstalling the app leaves them untouched.

Updates and support: https://github.com/brighinatommaso-eng/Converter
